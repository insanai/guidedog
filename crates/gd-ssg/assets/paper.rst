A first paper
=============

Claim
-----

For nonnegative :math:`n`, we have

.. math::

   \sum_{k=1}^n k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.

Derivation
----------

We pair the first and last terms; every pair sums to :math:`n+1`.
